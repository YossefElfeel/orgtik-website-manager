# Approach section direction

Mode: Persuade. This is a replacement of the rejected process component, not a new page or brand identity. Existing OrgTik copy, violet palette, Montserrat, assets, and single-page frontend scope remain fixed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Show one considered stage at a time instead of four equal columns with oversized numerals.

OWN-WORLD: OrgTik's dark aubergine surface, lavender accents, off-white type, open spacing, and supplied brand-application photography. No giant stage numbers, new cards, or decorative connector arrows.

STORY: Visitors scan Understand, Design, Deliver, and Evolve, then hover over a stage to read its original explanation. Tap and keyboard activation remain available.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A two-line heading leads into a roughly 40/60 split. Four tall typographic selectors occupy the left; a wide photographic frame and a balanced two-column narrative occupy the right. Mobile keeps a compact four-row selector above the image and narrative.

FORM: Local component redesign in the existing cinematic world, code-led; no seed round or new visual identity. The signature interaction is a hover-selected row coordinated with a photographic dissolve and narrative reveal. Keyboard arrows, Home, and End operate the stages; reduced-motion removes transition effects.

FINISH: The main agent inspected desktop and mobile layouts and verified hover, click, tap, and keyboard selection. The fresh read-only reviewer returned “ship” for code, interactions, and accessibility, without pixel approval. Scoped verification and build results are recorded in `design-qa.md`.

Preservation: The component inherits the existing Montserrat font and cinematic palette, uses the original narratives from `src/content.js`, and reuses `brand-glass.webp`, `brand-tablet.webp`, `brand-cards.webp`, and `brand-phone.webp` unchanged. Their supplied-source provenance remains in `public/assets/manifest.json`. This local component change does not introduce a global design-system rewrite, new `DESIGN.md`, or raster sidecars. The Impeccable engine is unavailable in this environment; project instructions and source remain the incumbent context.
